# UGC hooks, lines and creator briefs

For 6 second creator-style clips. Each line is about 12 to 18 words: a hook, then one true, visible detail. Works for this skill's Wireflow app, a real creator, or any talking-video tool.

## Hook patterns

| Pattern | Shape | Example (canvas tote) |
|---|---|---|
| Reaction | "Okay, <surprise>." | "Okay, this tote fits my laptop, my gym clothes and my lunch." |
| Confession | "I didn't think I needed <x>, but..." | "I didn't think I needed another bag, but this one goes everywhere." |
| You know when | "You know when <pain>?" | "You know when your bag strap digs in? Not with this one." |
| Show, don't tell | "Look how <detail>." | "Look how much fits in here. Laptop, jumper, water bottle." |
| Routine | "Every morning I <action>." | "Every morning I grab this and go. Done." |
| Swap | "I swapped <old> for this." | "I swapped my three tote bags for this one." |

Close with one short beat if words allow: "Finally, one bag." / "Obsessed." / "That's it."

## Writing rules

- Say only what is true and visible in the product. No health, money or results promises.
- No "I've used this for years" or "as a customer" from an AI creator.
- Contractions and plain words. Read it out loud: if it sounds like an ad, rewrite it.
- Put the line in double quotes when sending it to the app.

## Creator briefs

Describe a believable everyday person who matches the buyer, plus a real place and light.

| Buyer | Creator brief |
|---|---|
| Commuters, students | Woman in her early 20s, oversized hoodie, on a train platform bench, daylight, relaxed |
| Home cooks | Man in his 40s, apron over a t-shirt, home kitchen, warm evening light, friendly |
| Fitness | Woman in her 30s, gym top, sitting on a bench outside a gym, morning sun, energetic |
| Parents | Man in his 30s, casual shirt, living room with toys in the soft background, afternoon light, tired but smiling |
| Skincare | Woman in her late 20s, hair tied back, bathroom mirror, soft window light, calm |

Avoid real or famous people, children as the speaker, uniforms that suggest a profession (doctor, dentist) unless that is truthful and allowed, and any setting that implies a real customer review.

## Formats beyond 6 seconds

Longer UGC ads are usually several short clips cut together: hook clip, demo clip, payoff clip. Make each as its own run with the same creator brief, then edit them together with captions and music.

## Disclosure

Creators made with AI should be labelled as AI-generated where the platform asks for it. Do not present them as real customers or testimonials.
