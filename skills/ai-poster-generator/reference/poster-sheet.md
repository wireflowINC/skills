# Poster copy sheet and layout briefs

Use this sheet with or without Wireflow. Filled in, it is the approval document for the client and the brief for whoever makes the poster: the app or a designer.

## 1. Copy sheet

| Piece | Rule | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | 6 words or fewer, the name of the event or offer | "Rooftop Jazz Night" |
| Date | Weekday, day, month, time. Check the weekday against the calendar | "Saturday 21 November, 7pm" |
| Venue | Venue name, then street and number as the client prints it | "The Linden Hotel Rooftop, 12 Mill Street" |
| Call to action | One verb and one place: a short URL, phone or "at the door" | "Book tickets at lindenhotel.co/jazz" |
| Small print (optional) | One line, under 8 words: age limit, price, what is included | "Live quartet, cocktails, 18+" |

Checks before approval:

- Total under 35 words.
- The weekday matches the date and the year.
- The URL is written without `https://` and works when typed.
- Every price, discount or line-up comes from the client.
- The client has seen this exact wording and said yes.

## 2. Style line (shared by both layouts)

One or two sentences: the graphic style, the type feel, at most one illustration motif, a texture.

Examples:

- Art deco music poster, tall elegant serif, thin gold line art, paper grain.
- Bold retro screen print, chunky condensed sans serif, two flat colors, slight misregistration.
- Swiss minimal, big grotesk headline, one geometric shape, lots of white space.
- Hand-drawn market chalkboard, friendly script headline, simple food doodles.

## 3. Two layout briefs

Make them different enough that a client sees a real choice:

| Pattern | Headline | Artwork | Details |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic stack | Across the top third | Centered in the middle | Centered in the bottom third |
| Side column | Stacked on the left half | Small, or along an edge | A right-hand column, call to action in a band at the foot |
| Big art | Small, above the art | Fills two thirds | A strip along the bottom |
| Type only | Fills most of the poster | None, or one shape | Tucked under the headline |

## 4. Brief for a designer (no Wireflow)

- Size: 3:4 portrait for screens, or A3, A2 or US tabloid for print, with 3 mm bleed and a 5 percent safe margin.
- Copy: section 1, exactly as approved.
- Colors: background, headline, body text, hex codes.
- Style: section 2.
- Two options: the two layout briefs from section 3.
- Proof against the copy sheet letter by letter before print.
