# Infographic planner

Use this to turn source material into a copy deck and two layouts. It is also the free deliverable when Wireflow is not connected: the filled-in copy deck and layout sheet are enough for a designer to build the graphic in any design tool.

## Pick the structure

| Structure | Use it when the material is | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| Process | Steps that happen in order | 3 to 6 numbered steps |
| Comparison | Two options, tools or approaches | 2 columns, 3 to 5 rows of matched points |
| List | Tips, reasons, mistakes, with no order | 4 to 6 items |
| Timeline | Events tied to dates or phases | 3 to 6 dated points |
| Data | A few figures that tell one story | 3 to 6 figures, each with a label and a source |

If the material fits two structures, pick the one the reader needs to act on: a how-to is a process, a buying decision is a comparison.

## Write the copy deck

- Title: 8 words or fewer, says the takeaway, not the topic ("1 Blog Post, 7 Days of Content", not "Content Repurposing").
- Each section: a label of 2 to 5 words and one supporting line of 8 words or fewer.
- Figures: write them exactly as they should appear ("42%", "$1.2M", "3x"), with the unit. Only real figures from the person or their source.
- Source line for data: "Source: <publisher>, <year>". Footer: the brand or client name as plain text.
- Put every piece in double quotes. Only quoted words are drawn, so labels like "Title:" or "1." outside quotes stay off the image.

Fewer words mean cleaner lettering. Past about 60 words in total, split it into two graphics.

## Two layouts per structure

| Structure | Layout A | Layout B |
|---|---|---|
| Process | Numbered vertical list, number badge and icon on the left | Card grid (2 columns) or zigzag path with numbered cards |
| Comparison | Two columns with a header each and matched rows | Split page in two colors with a "vs" badge in the middle |
| List | Stacked rows with an icon each | Grid of square tiles |
| Timeline | Vertical line with dots and dates | Horizontal road or arrow with milestones |
| Data | Simple horizontal bar chart, figures at the bar ends | Big-number tiles in a grid, one figure per tile |

Write each layout as one sentence: the arrangement, where the numbers or icons sit, where the title and footer go.

## Style line

Background color, text color, one or two accent colors (hex codes if the brand has them), the font feel (bold geometric sans, rounded sans, serif headings) and the icon style (line icons, filled flat icons). Keep it to one or two sentences; the same style goes on both layouts.

## Sizes

The app renders 1728x2304 (3:4 portrait). That fits a LinkedIn or Instagram portrait post, a slide in portrait or a page in a PDF report. For a square or landscape version, plan it here and build it in a design tool.
