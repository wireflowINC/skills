# Model brief pack

Everything you need to get on-model photos from a flat-lay or ghost-mannequin garment photo. The briefs plug straight into this skill's Wireflow app (`model` and `lifestyle_setting`), and the three shot prompts at the end work in any image editor that accepts a reference photo.

## 1. Garment photo checklist

A better input photo means fewer drifts in the output.

- One garment per photo, the whole piece in frame, nothing cropped at the hem or sleeves.
- Front side up. Shoot the back separately if the back matters (open back, print on the back).
- Flat lay: smooth out wrinkles, straighten the seams, close the buttons and zips the way it is worn.
- Ghost mannequin: the cleanest option for fitted pieces, because it already shows the shape on a body.
- Plain light background, soft even daylight or two lights, no harsh shadows.
- True color: no filters, no warm or cool cast. Fix white balance before you upload.
- Sharp enough to see buttons, stitching and the print. Phone photos are fine at full resolution.
- Remove hangers, tags you do not want shown and anything else in the frame.
- Brand labels inside the neck may be copied or garbled by the model; hide them if you do not want them in the shot.

## 2. Model brief template

Fill in each part. Short, concrete words work better than adjectives.

> A <man / woman / person> in their <age range> with <build>, <skin tone>, <hair: length, texture, color>, <makeup or grooming>, <expression: relaxed, confident, smiling>, wearing <plain items that complete the outfit: white sneakers, straight dark jeans, black loafers>.

Rules:

- Pick the person your buyer will picture wearing it. For a size-inclusive range, write one brief per size group and run each.
- Keep extra pieces plain and neutral, and name them, so the model does not invent a jacket or bag.
- No real people's names, no "looks like" a celebrity.
- Keep the same brief across a collection so the store looks consistent.

### Ready briefs

| Store look | Model brief |
|---|---|
| Everyday womenswear | A woman in her early 30s, average build, warm brown skin, shoulder-length dark curly hair, natural makeup, relaxed confident expression, plain white low sneakers. |
| Menswear basics | A man in his late 20s, slim athletic build, light olive skin, short dark hair, clean shaven, calm friendly expression, straight dark blue jeans and plain white sneakers. |
| Plus size womenswear | A curvy woman in her 40s, size 18, light skin with freckles, auburn shoulder-length waves, soft smile, plain black ankle boots. |
| Streetwear | A person in their early 20s, lean build, deep brown skin, short twists, cool neutral expression, loose black cargo trousers and chunky white trainers. |
| Kids and teens | Use an adult model styled young, or skip models for kids' wear; many stores prefer flat lays for children's clothing. |

## 3. Lifestyle setting template

> <Doing what: walking, sitting, leaning> <where> in <time of day and light>, <one or two background details> softly blurred behind them.

| Garment | Setting |
|---|---|
| Summer dress | Walking along a sunny cobblestone street in a European old town in late afternoon light, a cafe terrace softly blurred behind her. |
| Knitwear | Sitting by a large window in a calm living room on a grey morning, a mug on the sill, soft diffused light. |
| Workwear | Standing in a bright modern office lobby, glass and pale wood behind, even daylight. |
| Swimwear | Standing on a pale sand beach at golden hour, gentle waves behind, warm low sun. |
| Outerwear | Walking through a city park in autumn, fallen leaves, overcast soft light. |

Keep the setting simple enough that the whole garment stays visible from collar to hem.

## 4. Three shot prompts (free fallback)

Use these in any image editor that takes the garment photo as a reference. Replace the parts in angle brackets.

**Rules (put first in every prompt):**

> Ecommerce fashion catalog photo of one adult model wearing the garment from the reference photo. Keep the garment exactly as photographed: same cut, length, fit, color, fabric texture, pattern, collar, buttons, pockets, seams, trims and any print or logo. Only the model, pose and background are new. Photorealistic, natural skin and hands, no text, no watermark. Model: <model brief>.

**Front:**

> Full-length front view, standing straight and facing the camera, arms relaxed at the sides, plain light grey seamless studio backdrop, soft even studio light, camera at waist height. 3:4.

**Three-quarter:**

> Full-length three-quarter view, body turned about 45 degrees, head toward the camera, one hand lightly at the waist, same grey backdrop and light. 3:4.

**Lifestyle:**

> Lifestyle photo, natural candid pose in motion, the full garment visible from collar to hem, setting: <lifestyle setting>. 3:4.

## 5. Check every result

- Color, fabric and print match the input.
- Collar, buttons, pockets, cuffs, belt and trims are all there and none were added.
- Length and fit look like the real piece. Compare the hem against the knee or hip in the flat lay.
- No extra bag, jacket, jewelry or logo you did not ask for.
- Hands, feet and face look natural.

If a detail drifts, say exactly what, and rerun only after deciding whether the redo is worth it.
