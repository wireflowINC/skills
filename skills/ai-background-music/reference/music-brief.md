# Music brief for ad and product videos

Use this to describe a background track so any text-to-music tool, or the Wireflow app, gets the same idea. It is also the free version when the Wireflow connector is not set up.

## The brief

```text
Video: <what the footage shows, and where it runs>
Cut length: <seconds>
Mood: <one line>
Genre and instruments: <style, two to four instruments>
Tempo: <BPM>
Shape: <how it builds: soft start, lift at the reveal, clean ending>
Under a voice: <yes / no>
Avoid: vocals, lyrics, humming, real artist or song names
```

## Mood and tempo by footage

| Footage | Mood line | Genre idea | BPM |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creator talking to camera, everyday product | Bright, friendly, easy | Indie pop, acoustic guitar, claps | 100 to 115 |
| Skincare, wellness, premium goods | Calm, soft, expensive | Ambient pads, felt piano, light pulse | 70 to 90 |
| Fast-cut launch or sale | Punchy, excited | House or electro pop, tight drums | 120 to 128 |
| App or SaaS screen demo | Clean, focused, optimistic | Minimal electronic, plucks, soft kick | 95 to 110 |
| Food, home, cosy lifestyle | Warm, relaxed | Lo-fi hip hop, warm keys, vinyl texture | 80 to 90 |
| Problem then fix | Tense, then a release | Cinematic pulse into a major lift | 90 to 110 |

## Leave room for a voice

- Ask for a sparse arrangement: drums, bass and pads, with no busy lead melody.
- Keep the low end steady and the middle open; the voice lives there.
- In the edit, duck the music about 10 to 15 dB under speech.

## Two prompts from one brief

Take A:

```text
Instrumental only, no vocals, no singing, no lyrics, no humming, no spoken words. <genre and instruments>, <mood>, <BPM> BPM. Background music for <video>. <shape>, about <cut length>, with a clean ending.
```

Take B: the same text plus "Same mood and tempo, but a different lead instrument and a different melody."

## Example

Brief: a 30 second UGC ad for a reusable water bottle, creator at the gym and then on a hike; bright, confident and warm; indie pop with acoustic guitar, light claps and soft synth pads.

Take A: "Instrumental only, no vocals, no singing, no lyrics, no humming, no spoken words. Indie pop with strummed acoustic guitar, light hand claps and soft synth pads, bright, confident and warm, 108 BPM. Background music for a UGC water bottle ad that moves from the gym to a mountain trail. Soft start, a lift when the scene goes outdoors, about 30 seconds, with a clean ending."

## Trim to the cut

Music models treat length as a hint. Plan to trim: start where the beat is clear, end on a bar line or add a half-second fade.
