# Storyboard planner

The free part of the skill: everything here works without Wireflow. Fill in the shot list, then sketch it, paste the frame prompts into any image tool, or send it through the app for six matching frames.

## Six beats for a short ad

| Panel | Beat | Typical shot | Seconds in a 30 s ad |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open: who and where | Wide establishing, hero's face visible | 0 to 4 |
| 2 | The problem | Close-up on the face or the trouble | 4 to 9 |
| 3 | The turn: they decide to act | Medium or over the shoulder | 9 to 14 |
| 4 | Help arrives or the product appears | Medium wide, two people or hero plus product | 14 to 20 |
| 5 | Proof: it works | Extreme close-up on the detail | 20 to 25 |
| 6 | Payoff and end shot | Wide or tracking, space left for the end card | 25 to 30 |

For 15 seconds merge beats 2 and 3; for 60 seconds run the app twice with the same style and character blocks (two quoted runs).

## Camera words the model understands

Extreme wide, wide, medium wide, medium, medium close-up, close-up, extreme close-up. Eye level, high angle, low angle, top down, over the shoulder, point of view, tracking from the side. Name the distance and angle first in every panel.

## The style block

One paragraph, sent with every frame. Pick one medium and keep it:

- Pitch sketch: "Clean storyboard sketch, black ink lines with soft grey marker shading and one accent colour, light paper texture."
- Client deck: "Cinematic colour storyboard frames, soft digital painting, muted palette with one warm highlight colour, gentle film grain."
- AI video brief: "Photoreal film still, 35mm lens look, natural light, shallow depth of field."

## The character block

Age, face, hair, skin, build, glasses or beard, and the full outfit with colours. Strong colours help: an orange jacket survives six frames better than "casual clothes". Write the same block every time you run the same hero.

## Other people

The app copies only the hero from panel 1. Describe anyone else fully in each panel they appear in, with the same words each time.

## Vertical ads

Frames come out 16:9. For a 9:16 Reels or TikTok ad, keep the action in the middle third of each frame and say "subject centred" in the panel, so a vertical crop still works for planning.
