# Sound effects cue sheet for ad and product clips

Plan the four effects with this before any run. It is also the free version when the Wireflow connector is not set up: the four prompts at the end paste into any text-to-sound-effects tool.

## The cue sheet

```text
Clip: <what it shows, length, where it runs>
Sound world: <space and echo>, <main materials>, <style: crisp, warm, cinematic, playful>

| Time  | Effect   | Moment                                   | Length |
|-------|----------|------------------------------------------|--------|
| 00:01 | whoosh   | <the cut, swipe or camera move>          | 1.5 s  |
| 00:09 | pop      | <the text, badge or sticker appearing>   | 1 s    |
| 00:04 | click    | <the button, lid, switch or tap>         | 1 s    |
| 00:00 | ambience | <the place and its quiet background>     | 6 s, loop |
```

## Which moment gets which effect

| On screen | Effect | Moment line idea |
|---|---|---|
| Jump cut, swipe, whip pan, push-in | whoosh | "Fast swipe from the unboxing to the product close-up" |
| Caption, price badge, sticker, emoji, logo | pop | "The '2 for 1' badge pops onto the screen" |
| App tap, toggle, lid, cap, latch, camera shutter | click | "A thumb taps the order button" |
| Kitchen, gym, office, street, bathroom, outdoors | ambience | "A bright kitchen in the morning: a kettle far off, birds outside" |
| Before and after reveal | whoosh | "Wipe from the dull before shot to the bright after shot" |
| Product drop into frame | pop | "The bottle drops onto the counter with a soft bounce" |

## Sound world by footage

| Footage | Sound world line |
|---|---|
| UGC selfie at home | Small room, little echo, soft fabric and wood, warm and natural |
| Premium skincare or candles | Quiet room, soft echo, glass and ceramic, gentle and polished |
| Tech or app demo | Dry close sound, plastic and glass, crisp and modern |
| Sports, gym, outdoor gear | Large bright room or open air, metal and rubber, punchy and energetic |
| Food and drink | Kitchen, short echo, ceramic, paper and liquid, warm and appetising |

## Mixing them in

- Start the whoosh 3 to 5 frames before the cut so it peaks on it.
- Put the pop and click on the exact frame of the action.
- Run the ambience under everything, 15 to 20 dB below the voice; loop it to the clip length.
- One effect per moment. Stacking two pops on one frame sounds cheap.

## Four prompts from one cue sheet

```text
Whoosh: Single short whoosh transition sound effect, clean airy swish that rises and cuts off, no music, no voice. Moment: <whoosh moment>. Sound world: <sound world>.
Pop: Single short pop sound effect, bright and satisfying, one hit with a quick tail, no music, no voice. Moment: <pop moment>. Sound world: <sound world>.
Click: Single short click sound effect, tight and precise, one click, no music, no voice. Moment: <click moment>. Sound world: <sound world>.
Ambience: Seamless background ambience sound bed, steady and low, no single loud events, no music, no voice. Setting: <ambience moment>. Sound world: <sound world>.
```

Set the tool's length to about 1.5, 1, 1 and 6 seconds, and switch on looping for the ambience if it offers it.

## Example

Clip: a 15 second soy candle ad, a hand lights the wick in a wooden living room at night, push-in on the flame, a 15% off sticker appears.
Sound world: quiet small room with soft echo, wood, glass and wax, warm and intimate.

Click prompt: "Single short click sound effect, tight and precise, one click, no music, no voice. Moment: The lighter clicks once to strike the flame. Sound world: quiet small room with soft echo, wood, glass and wax, warm and intimate."
