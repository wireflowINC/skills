# Product motion briefs

Copy, then replace `<product>` and the parts in angle brackets. Each brief is written for a 5 to 8 second image-to-video clip where the product photo is the first frame. Works in this skill's Wireflow app or any image-to-video model.

## By move

**Half orbit**
> Slow half orbit around the `<product>` from left to right at product height, soft studio light glides across it, subtle reflections, background stays clean, product shape and label unchanged.

**Light sweep**
> Static camera. A soft band of light sweeps slowly across the `<product>` from left to right, highlighting the `<material: glass, brushed metal, leather>`, then settles. Nothing else moves.

**Push-in to detail**
> Slow push-in toward the `<label / texture / stitching>` of the `<product>`, focus stays sharp on it, background softly blurs, calm and premium.

**Pull-back reveal**
> Start close on the `<detail>`, then a slow pull-back reveals the whole `<product>` on its surface, light stays soft and even.

**Turntable**
> The `<product>` rotates slowly on a round plinth, a quarter turn over the clip, camera static at product height, even studio light, clean background.

**In context**
> The `<product>` sits on `<surface in a real setting>`, the camera drifts gently past it from right to left, background elements slightly in motion (`<steam / leaves / curtains>`), product stays sharp and unchanged.

## By category

| Category | Move that works | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Skincare, perfume, bottles | Light sweep, half orbit | Glass warping, label letters changing |
| Shoes, bags | Turntable, half orbit | Laces or straps melting |
| Electronics | Push-in to ports or buttons, light sweep on the casing | Invented buttons or logos |
| Food and drink packs | In context with steam or ingredients | Packaging text drifting |
| Jewelry | Light sweep for sparkle, slow push-in | Stones multiplying |
| Furniture, decor | In-context drift, pull-back reveal | Proportions changing |

## Shot plan template (for a product page)

1. Hero: half orbit on white, square.
2. Detail: push-in to the material, square.
3. In use: in-context drift, vertical for social.

Three clips at about 48 credits each, about 144 credits.

## Before you render

- Clean first frame with margin around the product.
- One move only, slow.
- No text in the prompt; add captions later in an editor.
- The clip's shape follows the photo, so crop the photo to the target shape first.
