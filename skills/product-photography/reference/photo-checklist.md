# Input photo checklist

The result can only be as faithful as the photo you start from. A phone photo is fine if it passes these checks.

## Must have

- [ ] **One product**, whole, nothing cut off at the edges.
- [ ] **In focus**, especially the label and logo.
- [ ] **Even light**: no hard glare across the label, no deep shadow hiding a side.
- [ ] **Front view** (or the angle you want in every scene).
- [ ] **True colors**: shot in daylight or neutral light, no colored filters.

## Nice to have

- [ ] Plain, uncluttered background (a white wall or sheet of paper is enough).
- [ ] The product fills about two thirds of the frame.
- [ ] At least 1,000 pixels on the short side.
- [ ] A short written description of the product, to reuse in every scene.

## Quick phone setup

1. Put the product on a sheet of white paper that curves up a wall behind it.
2. Stand next to a window with daylight coming from the side. Turn off ceiling lights.
3. Hold the phone at the product's height, step back, and zoom in a little (2x) to avoid wide-angle distortion.
4. Tap the label to focus. Take a few shots and pick the sharpest.

## Common problems and what they cause

| Problem in the photo | What happens in the scenes |
|---|---|
| Blurry label | Letters get invented or scrambled |
| Strong reflections | Glare gets baked into every scene |
| Product cropped at the edge | The model guesses the missing part |
| Several products in one photo | Products merge or get dropped |
| Heavy color cast | Every scene inherits the wrong color |

## Rights

Use photos of products you sell or are allowed to show. Do not use another brand's product photos or images of people without permission.
