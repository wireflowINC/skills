# Product scene pack

Six standard scenes, each with a fill-in template. Replace `<product>` with a short, exact description of the product (for example "amber glass candle jar with an oak lid and a cream band label"). Works as a `scene` input for this skill's Wireflow app, or as an edit prompt in any image editor that takes a reference photo.

## 1. White background (marketplace main image)

> `<product>` centered on a seamless pure white background, soft even studio light from both sides, a gentle natural contact shadow under it, sharp focus on the whole product, nothing else in frame.

Use for: Amazon and Shopify main images, catalogs. Most marketplaces want the product to fill most of the frame on pure white; check your marketplace's current rules.

## 2. Lifestyle

> `<product>` in a <room or place> where it is naturally used, standing on <surface>, <time of day> light from <direction>, <one or two props> softly out of focus behind it, shallow depth of field, calm and inviting.

Use for: product page gallery, social posts, ads.

## 3. In hand

> A <who: e.g. woman's hand with short natural nails> holding `<product>` toward the camera, cropped at the wrist, <background> softly blurred, natural daylight, the product label facing the camera and fully readable.

Use for: showing size, social proof style ads. Ask who the buyer is and match the hand.

## 4. Flat lay

> Flat lay seen from directly above on <textured surface: linen, marble, kraft paper, wood>, `<product>` in the center, surrounded by <three props that fit the brand>, soft diffused daylight, neat balanced composition with negative space.

Use for: Instagram grids, bundles, gift guides.

## 5. Outdoor or context

> `<product>` on <outdoor surface: rock, sand, picnic blanket, café table> at <place>, <weather and light>, background with depth, editorial travel style.

Use for: products used outside (drinkware, sun care, outdoor gear, bags).

## 6. Colored studio backdrop

> `<product>` on a <bold color> seamless studio backdrop with a matching color floor, single hard light from the left casting a crisp shadow to the right, high contrast, modern ad look.

Use for: paid ads and banners that must stand out in a feed.

## Props by category

| Category | Props that work | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Beauty and skincare | Towels, ceramic dish, eucalyptus, water droplets, marble | Clutter that hides the label |
| Food and drink | Raw ingredients, wooden board, linen napkin, cutlery | Ingredients not in the product |
| Apparel and shoes | Folded clothes, sunglasses, keys, concrete or wood floor | Bodies cut at odd places |
| Electronics | Desk, notebook, plant, cable tidy, soft window light | Fake screens with text |
| Home and candles | Books, throw blanket, dried flowers, matches | A lit candle with its lid on |
| Jewelry | Velvet, silk, stone, a single flower | Busy patterns |

## Seasonal swaps

Add one line to any lifestyle or flat lay scene:

- Holidays: "warm fairy lights softly out of focus, pine sprigs, red and gold accents"
- Spring: "fresh tulips, pastel tones, bright morning light"
- Summer: "hard sun, blue sky, lemon slices, beach towel"
- Autumn: "fallen leaves, knit blanket, warm amber light"

## Keep the product honest

- Never let a scene add claims: no badges, awards, "organic" stamps or prices unless they are on the real product.
- If the label has words, ask for the label to face the camera and check every letter in the result.
- Keep scale believable: a 10 cm jar should not tower over a coffee cup.
