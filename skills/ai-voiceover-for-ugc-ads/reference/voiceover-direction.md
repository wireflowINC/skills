# Voiceover direction for UGC ads

Use this to write a script that sounds spoken, fits the footage, and can be handed to any voice: the Wireflow app, another TTS tool or a human voice actor.

## Word budget

Speech runs at about 2.5 words a second (calm reads closer to 2.2, hype reads closer to 3).

| Footage | Words | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| 6 s | 12 to 15 | Hook + one proof |
| 10 s | 20 to 25 | Hook + proof + call to action |
| 15 s | 30 to 38 | Hook + two proofs + call to action |
| 30 s | 65 to 75 | Hook + problem + 2 or 3 proofs + offer + call to action |

Leave half a second of silence at the end so the cut does not clip the last word.

## Make it sayable

- Hook in the first five words: a reaction ("Okay, wait."), a confession ("I almost returned this."), or a "you know when..." moment.
- Short sentences. One idea each. Contractions ("it's", "I've").
- Numbers and symbols as spoken: "20%" becomes "twenty percent", "2x" becomes "twice", "$29" becomes "twenty-nine dollars".
- A comma where a breath goes; a full stop where the edit cuts.
- Cut stacked adjectives and lists of three features; pick the one the footage shows.
- Read it out loud once. Any word you trip on, change.

## Tone lines that work

| Tone line | Use for |
|---|---|
| Casual and upbeat, like a friend recommending it | Lifestyle products, TikTok and Reels |
| Calm and confident, unhurried | Skincare, wellness, premium goods |
| Excited but not shouting, quick pace | Launches, limited offers |
| Warm and reassuring | Services, apps that solve a worry |
| Dry and matter-of-fact, a little funny | Problem-solution demos, B2B tools |

## Direction sheet (for a human voice actor or another TTS tool)

```text
Script: <final words>
Length: <N> words, about <N / 2.5> seconds, footage is <length>
Tone: <tone line>
Pace: <slow / natural / quick>
Emphasis: <the one or two words to lean on>
Pauses: <after the hook, before the call to action>
Pronunciation: <brand or product names, spelled the way they sound>
Do not: add words, change the offer, read the hashtags
```

## Example

Written: "Our tote has a padded 15" laptop sleeve, 3 internal pockets & water-resistant canvas!!! 20% OFF this week only."

Spoken (about 8 seconds): "Okay, this tote fits my laptop, my gym clothes and my lunch. Finally, one bag. Twenty percent off this week."

## Honest by design

- Keep every claim, price and offer exactly as the brand gave it.
- A synthetic voice is not a customer. Do not script it as a testimonial ("I've used this for years") unless the words come from a real customer who agreed.
- Never copy a real person's or a celebrity's voice.
