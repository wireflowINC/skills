# Presenter scripts and casting for HeyGen Video product clips

A presenter product video is one person, facing the camera, holding the product and saying one short thing about it. This sheet is useful on its own: the scripts work for a real person filming on a phone, for the Wireflow app, or for any talking-presenter tool.

## Script length

The app makes an 8 second clip. That fits about 15 to 20 spoken words at a relaxed pace. Fewer words sound calmer; more get rushed or cut.

## Structure for 8 seconds

1. **Name it (2 to 3 seconds).** What the product is, in the words a buyer uses. "This is the serum I use every night."
2. **One true detail (4 to 5 seconds).** Something a viewer can picture and the brand can stand behind: how it is used, what is in the box, what it fits, how it feels. "Three drops, pat it in, and that is my whole routine."

No price, discount, rating or result claim unless the seller gave it to you and it is true today.

## Script patterns

| Pattern | Example (about 18 words) |
|---|---|
| The routine | "This is the serum I use every night. Three drops, pat it in, and that is my whole routine." |
| What fits | "This tote holds my laptop, my gym clothes and my lunch, and it still zips shut at the top." |
| The detail | "Look at the stitching on this wallet. Every seam is done by hand, and the leather softens as you use it." |
| The swap | "I replaced three bottles on my shelf with this one cleanser. Morning and night, same bottle, nothing else." |
| The gift | "If you need a gift for someone who loves coffee, this grinder is quiet, small, and looks great on a counter." |
| The how-to | "Twist the cap, press once, and you get exactly one dose. No measuring, no mess, no guessing." |
| The unboxing line | "Inside the box: the bottle, a refill pouch and a little cloth bag to carry it. That is everything." |

Write two or three options and let the person pick. Read each one out loud once: if it takes longer than 8 seconds at a calm pace, cut it.

## Casting the presenter

Describe an everyday person who fits the buyer, in one or two sentences:

- Age range and look: "a man in his 40s with a short grey beard".
- Clothes that suit the product: "a navy overshirt", "gym top".
- Where they stand: "a bright kitchen with a wooden counter", "a bathroom with white tiles and a plant".
- Mood: "relaxed and friendly", "calm and matter of fact".

Do not describe a real or famous person, a child, or a specific influencer. The presenter is AI-made; never present them as a real customer.

## Pairings that work

| Product type | Presenter and place |
|---|---|
| Skincare, haircare | Woman or man in their 20s to 40s, bathroom or bedroom vanity, soft window light |
| Kitchen and coffee | Home cook in an apron or knitwear, kitchen counter, morning light |
| Fitness and outdoor | Person in sportswear, a park path or a home gym corner |
| Tech accessories | Person at a tidy desk, laptop behind them, cool daylight |
| Fashion accessories | Person by a window or on a quiet street, natural light |
| Pet products | Person on a living room floor or sofa (no animal in the brief unless you want one generated) |

## Checks before you run

- The product photo is the real product, sharp, with the label or logo readable.
- The script is 15 to 20 words, every claim true.
- The presenter description names no real person.
- The person knows the clip is AI and will label it where the ad platform asks.
