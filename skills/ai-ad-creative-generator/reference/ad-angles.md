# Ad angles and headline patterns

Use different angles for different concepts. Variants of one angle belong inside one concept as alternative headlines.

## Seven angles

| Angle | The question it answers | Headline pattern | Example (terracotta mug) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ritual | How does it fit my day? | "<Moment>, made better" | "Slow mornings, made better" |
| Outcome | What do I get? | "<Result> without <pain>" | "Café coffee without the queue" |
| Problem / solution | Why do I need it? | "Tired of <problem>?" | "Tired of chipped mugs?" |
| Product hero | What is it? | "<Material/feature>, made to <use>" | "Stoneware, made for every day" |
| Offer | Why now? | "<Real offer> on <product>" | "Free shipping on every mug" (only if true) |
| Old way vs new | Why switch? | "Swap <old> for <new>" | "Swap paper cups for this" |
| Gift | Who is it for? | "For the <person> who <trait>" | "For the friend who lives on coffee" |

## Layout briefs that render well

- **Headline top, product center, CTA bottom.** Safest for 9:16; keep the headline in the upper third but below the top 14%.
- **Split layout.** Product on one side, headline stacked on the other. Strong in 1:1 and 4:5.
- **Product on a solid color field.** One bold brand color, big type, minimal props. Stands out in a busy feed.
- **Lifestyle photo with a text band.** A real-looking scene with a solid band of color holding the headline.

## Type and copy rules

- 2 to 6 words per headline, 2 to 3 per button.
- One typeface family per concept; name it loosely ("bold modern serif", "rounded sans-serif").
- High contrast between text and background.
- Quote every word exactly in the brief. Anything unquoted may be invented by the model.
- No prices, discounts, guarantees, ratings or "#1" unless real and allowed by the ad platform.

## Placement safe areas

| Ratio | Use | Keep text out of |
|---|---|---|
| 1:1 | Feed | Outer 5% |
| 4:5 | Feed, portrait | Outer 5% |
| 9:16 | Stories, Reels, TikTok | Top 14%, bottom 20%, right 10% (buttons and captions) |

## Test plan

1. Round 1: 3 concepts (3 different angles), one headline each, all three ratios. 9 ads.
2. Keep the concept with the best cost per result.
3. Round 2: 3 headline variants of the winning concept. 9 more ads.

Change one thing per round so you know what made the difference.
