# Listing slot templates

Fill the `<...>` parts. Quote every word that must appear in the image. Works as the `slot_brief` input of this skill's Wireflow app, or as a brief for a designer or any image editor that takes a reference photo.

## 1. Main image

> Main image: the `<product>` alone, upright, centered on a pure white background (RGB 255,255,255), filling about 85% of the frame, soft even studio light, a gentle contact shadow, no text, no props, no packaging unless the product is sold in it.

Checks: white is truly white; nothing but the product; the product is large. This is a generated draft: the seller checks Amazon's current main-image rules before using it as the main image.

## 2. Feature infographic

> Feature infographic: the `<product>` on the right half against a soft `<light color>` background, with `<3 or 4>` short callouts on the left, each with a thin line pointing to the matching part and a simple outline icon. Callout text, exactly: '`<feature 1>`', '`<feature 2>`', '`<feature 3>`'. Clean sans-serif type in dark grey, generous spacing, no other words.

Callout writing: three to five words, a noun first ("Leakproof screw lid", "Fits 13-inch laptops"). Facts only, supplied by the seller.

## 3. Lifestyle

> Lifestyle: the `<product>` in use in `<setting the buyer recognizes>`, `<who, if anyone, and what they are doing>`, natural `<time of day>` light, the product sharp and clearly visible, background softly out of focus, no text.

## 4. Size and scale

> Size and scale: the `<product>` on a light background with thin dimension lines and labels, exactly: '`<height>`', '`<width>`'. Next to it, for scale, a `<familiar object: smartphone, hand, coffee mug>`. Clean diagram style, no other words.

Use real measurements only. If they are unknown, use the familiar object without numbers.

## 5. Detail close-up

> Detail close-up: macro photo of the `<part: stitching, nozzle, texture, clasp>` of the `<product>`, shallow depth of field, crisp texture, soft side light, no text.

## 6. What's in the box

> What's in the box: flat lay seen from above on a light background, showing `<item 1>`, `<item 2>` and `<item 3>` neatly arranged, each with a small label, exactly: '`<label 1>`', '`<label 2>`', '`<label 3>`'.

Show only what is actually included.

## 7. How to use

> How to use: three panels in a row, numbered 1, 2, 3, showing `<step 1 action>`, `<step 2 action>` and `<step 3 action>` with the `<product>`, short captions under each panel, exactly: '`<caption 1>`', '`<caption 2>`', '`<caption 3>`'.

## 8. Comparison (own range only)

> Comparison: the `<product>` in `<size or version A>` and `<size or version B>` side by side on a light background, a simple two-column table underneath with the rows, exactly: '`<row label>`: `<A value>` / `<B value>`'.

Compare the seller's own versions. Do not show or name competitors.

## Copy rules for every slot

- Facts from the seller only. No "best", "#1", "clinically proven", awards or star ratings unless they can be proven and the marketplace allows them.
- Short words render best. Long sentences come out garbled.
- Same font style and color across all text slots, so the gallery looks like one brand.
- Read every rendered word before approving.
