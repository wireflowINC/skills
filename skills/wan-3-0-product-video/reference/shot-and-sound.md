# Shot and sound briefs for Wan 3.0 product clips

Wan 3.0 makes the picture and the sound in one pass, so every brief has two halves: what the camera and the scene do, and what you hear. This sheet works on its own: paste a brief into the `shot_and_sound` input of the Wireflow app, or into any start-frame video tool that makes audio.

## How to write one

Four parts, in this order, one or two sentences in all:

1. **The move.** One slow camera move: push-in, pull-back, half orbit, slide past, or a locked-off camera.
2. **What moves in the scene.** Something the product or its setting really does: a flame, bubbles, steam, a fabric edge in a breeze, light crossing a wall.
3. **The sound of that motion.** Name the sound the motion makes, close and specific: "soft crackle of the wick", "fine fizz of bubbles", "a low hum".
4. **The room.** One quiet background: room tone, distant birds, light rain on a window, a cafe murmur far off.

Leave out music and voices. The app already asks for no music and no speech; a music bed is better added in an editor, where you can choose it.

## Briefs by product

| Product | Shot and sound brief |
|---|---|
| Candle | Slow push-in toward the lit candle, the flame flickers gently and the wick crackles softly, warm light drifts across the wall, quiet room tone. |
| Sparkling drink (can or glass) | Locked-off close shot of the glass beside the can, bubbles rise steadily through the drink, fine fizz and a single ice cube settling with a light clink, quiet kitchen tone. |
| Coffee, tea | Slow slide past the cup on the counter, steam curls up from the surface, a soft kettle hiss fading in the background, morning room tone. |
| Skincare bottle | Slow half orbit around the bottle on a wet stone, small water droplets run down the stone, gentle drips and a faint trickle, calm spa room tone. |
| Perfume | Slow push-in on the bottle on a vanity, light from a window moves across the glass, a soft fabric rustle as a curtain shifts, quiet bedroom tone. |
| Headphones, speakers | Locked-off shot of the product on a desk, its small status light pulses once, a low hum from the desk, faint keyboard taps far off. |
| Mechanical keyboard | Slow push-in along the keys, the backlight sweeps left to right, crisp key clicks from off screen, quiet office tone. |
| Outdoor gear (bottle, bag, boots) | Slow pull-back from the product on a rock by a stream, leaves move in a light breeze, running water and distant birds. |
| Kitchen tool | Slow half orbit around the tool on a wooden board, a light dusting of flour drifts down, a soft tap of wood, kitchen room tone. |
| Fashion item | Slow slide past the garment on a hanger by an open window, the hem lifts slightly in a breeze, fabric rustle, street sounds far below. |

## When sound does not help

Some products make no sound and sit in silent settings: jewelry on velvet, a phone case on white, a print on a wall. A Wan clip still works, but you are paying for audio you will probably mute. For those, a silent start-frame clip from a cheaper model is the better buy; say so before quoting.

## Shot plan for a product page

| Clip | Purpose | Brief pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | First thing on the page | Push-in on the product in its best setting, the setting's signature sound |
| Detail | Show the material | Locked-off close shot, light crossing the surface, one quiet texture sound |
| In use, without hands | Show what it does | The product's own motion (fizz, steam, flame, light), the sound of that motion |

## Checks before you run

- The start frame is the product's real photo, sharp, label readable, in the shape you want out (square in gives square out).
- The brief names one move only.
- The sound comes from something visible in the shot or a believable room.
- Nothing in the brief asks the product to do what it cannot do.
